The **Copper Cobblestone Generator** is the first tier. It produces one cobblestone every four seconds and sends its output directly into an adjacent container. Each of the five upgrades uses the previous generator as an ingredient.

## Crafting the Generator

Make the copper tier at a crafting table with **4 copper ingots, 2 obsidian, 1 glass, 1 water bucket and 1 lava bucket**. The recipe returns two empty buckets.

| Left | Center | Right |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copper ingot | Obsidian | Copper ingot |
| Water bucket | Glass | Lava bucket |
| Copper ingot | Obsidian | Copper ingot |

For an upgrade, put the previous generator in the center and surround it with eight of the material shown below.

## Generator Tiers

| Tier | Upgrade material | Cobblestone per cycle | Cycle at 20 ticks per second | Cobblestone per second |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Copper | Starter recipe | 1 | 4 s | 0.25 |
| Iron | Iron ingots | 1 | 2 s | 0.5 |
| Gold | Gold ingots | 1 | 1 s | 1 |
| Emerald | Emeralds | 1 | 0.5 s | 2 |
| Diamond | Diamonds | 2 | 0.5 s | 4 |
| Netherite | Netherite scrap | 10 | 0.5 s | 20 |

These rates describe the generator while it is ticking and has space to store or deliver its output.

## How It Works

Place a chest or barrel against the face with the output port. If the container is missing or runs out of room, the generator stores up to **64 cobblestone**. Interact with an empty main hand to collect the stored cobblestone, or free space in the connected container so the generator can transfer it on its next cycle.
