Dorios’ Basics adds tools and workstations for making early resources from common blocks. Dig up fragments with a hammer, sift blocks for materials and make water or lava as your world expands.

## Pebbles

Sneak and interact with grass or dirt while holding a hammer to search for stone pebbles, gravel fragments, dirt, sand and other materials. Mud can yield Mud Balls. Red Sand and Terracotta can yield Red Sand Handfuls. Use the crafting recipes for the fragments you collect to turn them into full blocks.

![Pebbles and other materials dug up with a wooden hammer](/img/addons/basics/hammer_use.jpg)

## Hammers

Mine the input block directly with a hammer to convert it. A wooden hammer is enough to start the Cobblestone → Gravel → Dirt → Sand chain.

| Input | Hammer drop |
| --- | --- |
| Cobblestone | Gravel |
| Gravel | Dirt |
| Dirt | Sand |
| Deepslate | Cobbled Deepslate |
| Cobbled Deepslate | Crushed Cobbled Deepslate |
| Netherrack | Crushed Netherrack |
| Blackstone | Crushed Blackstone |
| End Stone | Crushed End Stone |

## Flint Knife

Arrange **3 gravel** in an L to craft flint. Place the flint diagonally above a stick to make the **Flint Knife**. Break leaves with it for a chance to get **Plant Fiber**, then craft four fibers in a 2×2 square to make a string.

## Mortar — Getting Water

Craft a Mortar with a **Stone Pebble** between two cobblestone and another cobblestone below it. At a crafting table, put **2 saplings** in a row, with the **Mortar** and a **Glass Bottle** on the row underneath. This gives you a Water Bottle and returns the Mortar.

![Water Bottle recipe with two saplings, a Mortar and a glass bottle](/img/addons/utilitycraft/recipe_water_mortar.jpg)

## Sieve

1. Use a mesh on the Sieve to equip it.
2. Hold a siftable block, such as gravel, and interact with the Sieve to add it.
3. Keep interacting to sift the block and collect the results. The input block and mesh tier determine what can drop.
4. To remove the mesh, sneak and interact with an empty hand.

An interaction also works on sieves in a **5×5** square centered on the sieve you use, at the same height. Bring enough blocks to fill the whole group.

![Several sieves on the same level being used as a group](/img/addons/utilitycraft/using_multiple_sieves.jpg)

## Crucible

Place a heat source directly under the Crucible and add **4 cobblestone**. Each cobblestone melts into a quarter bucket of lava. When it is full, use an empty bucket to collect the lava or a Water Bucket to turn it into obsidian.

| Heat source | Relative heat |
| --- | ---: |
| Torch | 1 |
| Fire, Campfire, Magma Block, Big Torch | 2 |
| Soul Fire, Soul Torch, Soul Campfire | 3 |
| Lava | 4 |
| Blaze Block | 6 |

## Sink

The Sink provides an infinite source of water for compatible UtilityCraft fluid setups.
